# ai-lab-signals

Remote mirror of verified local AI lab signal artifacts.

- `blog-scout-*`: raw signal scans from curated feeds
- `signal-to-offer-*`: offer ranking results from signal targets
- `content-assets-*`: daily X drafts, landing snippets, email drafts
- `yield.json`: 7-day feed monetization yield tracking
- `winrate.json`: 7-day offer win rates from ranking history
- `ledger.json`: rolling ledger of recent runs

No secrets or credentials are published here.
